Talldungen is a restaurant and hotel located in Brösarp, Southern Sweden. It is a warm and generous gathering place for great food and wine surrounded by breathtaking nature.

Developed in collaboration with design studio Malmsten Hellberg.

https://talldungen.se

3rd Cycle Research is a postgraduate programme at the Department of Performing Arts and Film at Zürich University of the Arts. The programme is a space for artistic research and experimentation in the field of contemporary performing arts.

The website is both a place to learn about the programmes and a platform for the students to present their work.

Built in collaboration with designer Maryam Fanni.

https://3rd-ipf-ddk.zhdk.ch

Teg Publishing is a publisher and media production company publishing poetry, music, documentaries and podcasts.

Developed in collaboration with design studio Malmsten Hellberg.

https://tegpublishing.se

Komm, The Swedish Association of Communication Agencies, is a trade association for companies that work with marketing and communication in Sweden.

Design by Asso, identity by Open Studio, and development by me.

https://komm.se

WWF Matguiden is a mobile app that brings together WWF’s three consumer guides: the Fish Guide, the Meat Guide, and the Vego Guide. It helps you make smarter, more sustainable food choices, calculate the environmental impact of your diet, and explore delicious, eco-friendly recipes.

The app is built using React Native.

Apple App Store
Google Play Store
https://www.wwf.se/matguiden

Hyper Human is an exhibition at the National Museum of Science and Technology in Stockholm.

Visitors are challenged by a range of ethical questions about technical and medical development at three stations. I helped develop these in collaboration with Spektra.

The graphical effects on the transparent touchscreens were created using WebGL.

https://tekniskamuseet.se/hyper-human

Beckmans College of Design’s degree exhibition show Formation with projects made by the graduating students from the classes of 2022.

Design by Eric Rösmark. Built using Next.js and Prismic.

https://2022.beckmans.college

The Swedish Archive for Queer Moving Images is a platform and a digital archive for queer moving images. The site is full of news, events, videos, podcasts and more about the queer film world.

Web design by Sakaria Studio and identity by Maryam Fanni.

https://saqmi.se

Ascape is a small but great architecture office in Stockholm.

https://www.ascape.se

Minna Sakaria is a senior graphic designer and design educator. Under the name Sakaria Studio she designs artistic visual identities for brands, exhibitions and individuals.

Minna provided the design and I developed this website.

https://www.sakaria.se

I like building solid things. Things that last, things that solve real problems that need solving. I like my websites loading fast and the pace we travel the internet to be slow.

The tools I use most at the moment are React, React Native, Next.js, Node, MongoDB, Sanity and Wordpress but I’m used to working with just about anything to make things work.
